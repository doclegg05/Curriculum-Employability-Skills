Introduction

What Is Assertive Communication?

Assertive communication is expressing your needs, opinions, and feelings honestly and directly, without attacking others.

→Clear and direct
→Calm tone of voice
→Respects both people
→Open to listening
🙋

Respect for yourself and for others.

Introduction

The Four Styles

Passive
→Avoids conflict
→Puts others first
→Says “It's fine” when it isn't
Aggressive
→Blames or threatens
→Talks over others
→“You always mess this up!”
Passive-Aggressive
→Hides anger
→Sarcasm or silent treatment
→“Fine, whatever you want.”
Assertive
→Honest and calm
→Respects everyone
→“I need help finishing this by noon.”

Assertive Language

The words you choose change the conversation.

Presentation 1

Use “I” Statements

“I” statements describe your experience instead of blaming the other person.

→I feel… (the emotion)
→when… (the specific behavior)
→because… (the effect on you)
→I would like… (the request)
💡

“I feel rushed when reports come in late because I can't check them. I'd like them by 3:00.”

Communicating Assertively

Presentation 1

“You” vs. “I” Statements

Same problem, different result.

“You” Statement
→“You never listen to me.”
→“You're always late.”
→“You made me look bad.”
“I” Statement
→“I feel unheard when I'm interrupted.”
→“I get worried when meetings start late.”
→“I felt embarrassed when my mistake was shared.”

Presentation 1

The DESC Script

A four-part plan for a hard conversation.

1

Describe

State the facts of the situation.

2

Express

Share how it affects you.

3

Specify

Ask for a specific change.

4

Consequences

Explain the positive result of the change.

Saying No Respectfully

Four ways to decline without damaging the relationship.

Direct

Simple No

“I can't take that on today.”

Offer Options

Alternative

“I can't today, but I can on Thursday.”

Ask for Priorities

Trade-Off

“Which task should I set aside for this?”

Take Time

Delay

“Let me check my schedule and get back to you.”

Check Your Understanding

Three quick questions.

Exit Ticket · Question 1 of 3

Which statement is assertive?

A“Whatever, it's fine.”
B“You never do your share!”
C“I need your part by noon so I can finish.”
D“Must be nice to leave early.”
Check answer ▶

✅ C. “I need your part by noon so I can finish.”  It is direct, specific, and respectful.

APassive
BAggressive
CPassive-aggressive
DAssertive

✅ C. Passive-aggressive  The words agree, but the tone shows hidden anger.
