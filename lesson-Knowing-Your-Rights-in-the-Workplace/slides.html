→A safe workplace
→Freedom from discrimination

Introduction

Key Federal Laws

💵

FLSA

Fair Labor Standards Act: minimum wage, overtime, and child labor rules.

🦺

OSH Act

Occupational Safety and Health Act: the right to a safe workplace.

⚖

Title VII

Civil Rights Act: no discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, or national origin.

♿

ADA

Americans with Disabilities Act: equal opportunity and reasonable accommodations.

Your Pay Rights

💲

Minimum Wage

You must earn at least the federal or state minimum, whichever is higher.

⏱

Overtime

Most hourly workers earn 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek.

🧾

Pay Records

Keep records of your hours and pay to check for errors.

Safety and Fair Treatment

A safe job, free from discrimination.

Presentation 2

Your Right to a Safe Workplace

⚠

Know the Hazards

Employers must train you about hazards on the job.

🥽

Protective Gear

Employers must provide required safety equipment, generally at no cost.

📞

Report Concerns

You can file a complaint with OSHA.

🚫

No Retaliation

You can't be punished for reporting a safety issue.

Knowing Your Rights in the Workplace

Presentation 2

Discrimination and Harassment

It is illegal to treat employees or job applicants unfairly because of a protected characteristic.

→Race, color, national origin
→Religion
→Sex, including pregnancy
→Age (40 and older)
→Disability
→Genetic information

Enforced by the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC).

Evaluation

Check Your Understanding

Three quick questions.

Exit Ticket · Question 1 of 3

You're an hourly, non-exempt employee who works 46 hours this week. How are the extra 6 hours paid?

ARegular pay
BTime and a half
CNot paid; it's expected
DComp time only
Check answer ▶

✅ B. Time and a half  Hours over 40 in a workweek are paid at 1.5 times your regular rate.

Exit Ticket · Question 2 of 3

Which agency handles workplace discrimination complaints?

AOSHA
BIRS
CEEOC
DFDA
Check answer ▶

✅ C. EEOC  The EEOC enforces federal anti-discrimination laws.

Exit Ticket · Question 3 of 3

ANormal scheduling
BIllegal retaliation
CYour fault
