Check Your Understanding

Image placeholder

Exit Ticket · Question 1 of 3

You're running 15 minutes late for your shift. What is the most professional choice?

AArrive and say nothing
BContact your supervisor before your shift starts
CSkip the shift
DAsk a coworker to cover without telling anyone
Check answer ▶

✅ B. Contact your supervisor before your shift starts  Letting your supervisor know ahead of time shows responsibility.

Professionalism and Diversity

Exit Ticket · Question 2 of 3

Which is an example of inclusive behavior?

AOnly eating lunch with your friends
BInviting a new coworker to join your group
CTelling jokes about other cultures
DSpeaking for others in meetings
Check answer ▶

✅ B. Inviting a new coworker to join your group  Including others helps everyone feel welcome.

AHide it
