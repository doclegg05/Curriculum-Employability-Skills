Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest happens when personal gain could affect your work decisions.

→Favoring a friend or relative
→Accepting gifts from vendors
→Working for a competitor on the side
→Using work contacts for a personal business

Evaluation

Check Your Understanding

Three quick questions.

Exit Ticket · Question 1 of 3

Which is an example of time theft?

ATaking your scheduled lunch
BRunning personal errands on paid time
CWorking approved overtime
DLeaving at the end of your shift
Check answer ▶

✅ B. Running personal errands on paid time  Paid time should be spent on work.

Workplace Ethics

Exit Ticket · Question 2 of 3

A vendor offers you an expensive gift. What should you do?

AAccept it quietly
BCheck the gift policy and tell your supervisor
CShare it with coworkers
DAsk for a bigger gift
Check answer ▶

✅ B. Check the gift policy and tell your supervisor  Gifts from vendors can create a conflict of interest.

Workplace Ethics

Exit Ticket · Question 3 of 3

Which question helps test an ethical decision?

AWill I get caught?
BWould I be comfortable if it were made public?
CIs everyone else doing it?
DWill it save me time?
Check answer ▶

✅ B. Would I be comfortable if it were made public?  If you wouldn't want it public, rethink the choice.
