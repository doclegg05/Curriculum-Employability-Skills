Introduction

What Is Money Management?

Money management is planning how you earn, spend, save, and protect your money.

→Know what comes in
→Know what goes out
→Plan before you spend
→Save for the unexpected
📒

A plan turns income into choices.

Presentation 1

The 50/30/20 Guideline

A simple starting point for dividing take-home pay.

🏠

50% Needs

Rent, utilities, groceries, transportation, insurance.

🎉

30% Wants

Dining out, streaming, hobbies, new clothes.

🏦

20% Savings & Debt

Emergency fund, savings goals, extra debt payments.

Money Management

Presentation 1

Fixed vs. Variable Expenses

Fixed costs stay the same each month. Variable costs change.

Fixed
→Rent or mortgage
→Car payment
→Insurance
→Phone plan
Variable
→Groceries
→Gas
→Utilities
→Entertainment

Understanding Your Credit Score

Lenders, landlords, and some employers use your credit score to judge how reliably you repay.

→Keep card balances low
→Don't open many accounts at once
