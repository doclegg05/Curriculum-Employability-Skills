Adult education instructor teams

Your next team meeting with Bespoke

Use Bespoke to choose how your lesson will look: its colors, fonts, backgrounds, title, section dividers and cards. Discuss the preview together during a Teams call. Choose Save when you agree, Open when you come back, and Send to Britt when the look is ready for review. You do not need to write a lesson, know code, sign in, or pick a folder.

Open Bespoke

Set up once

Ask Britt for the Bespoke website link. Pin it in your Teams channel. Choose one spokesperson to operate Bespoke.

On How to use Bespoke, the spokesperson sets an edit code of at least 4 characters and writes it down. That code is how the team opens the design later. It is a convenience lock, not an account. There is no sign-in.

At the start of each call

  1. Open the Bespoke website. Choose Open. Pick your lesson and enter the edit code. Check the lesson name and the team’s previous choices. For your first meeting, choose Next and enter your lesson and spokesperson instead.
  2. Share the browser window in your Teams call. The spokesperson changes the options as the team decides; everyone watches the same preview.

Choose the look together

  1. Theme preset: pick the closest starting look. You can keep it as it is, or adjust the options below.
  2. Color lead and background: try the main color, sidebar and background texture. The choices come from the approved SPOKES library.
  3. Title and dividers: compare the opening slide and section-divider layouts. Use the Title and Divider preview tabs to see them.
  4. Cards and fonts: compare card shapes and typefaces. Use the Cards preview tab. Leave Vary by chapter off for one consistent card style, or turn it on to compare different chapter styles.
  5. Try sample text: optionally change a title or a few sample lines to see how they fit. The supplied text is enough to choose a look. Nothing here requires a completed lesson.
  6. Review: check the summary together and agree which look to keep. On a small screen, use Design and Preview to switch between controls and the sample.

Before you end the call

  1. Choose Save. The design is kept for the next computer that has the lesson name and the edit code.
  2. In Teams, note what the team decided. At the next meeting, the spokesperson chooses Open. Let one spokesperson save each revision.

When the team is ready for review

Choose Send to Britt. She receives a draft to review. You do not sign in, and you do not choose a folder. Sending does not build the lesson.

Britt reviews the selected look. Those approved choices guide the later lesson build, and your team checks that the finished lesson displays them correctly. A preview in Bespoke is a design sample.

Save, Open, and Send to Britt are the steps you need. The browser also keeps a convenience copy on this computer. A builder can still download a design file; that download is not your main step.

If something goes wrong

  • Your browser draft is missing: choose Open and enter the lesson and edit code.
  • The code is refused: try the code you wrote down. The page says the code is not right when it does not match.
  • You opened the wrong design: choose Recover previous draft, or Open again.
  • Another tab changed the draft: choose Save to keep this tab’s work, close extra Bespoke tabs, then reload.
  • Save says it is not connected: tell Britt. She connects saving once. Until then, a builder can download the design file.

Optional view links

A view link shows one moment in the design. It will not show later changes. It also contains the sample text and team contact details, so share it only with intended reviewers. The edit code is a convenience lock; it is not a private account. Save and Open are the simplest way to continue next time.
