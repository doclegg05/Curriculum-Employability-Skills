Adult education instructor teams

Choose your lesson look together

BeSpoke helps your team choose the look of five reusable slide types: Title, Chapter divider, Text boxes, Video, and Activity. Share the preview during a Teams call and agree on the design together. The words and slides are samples. You do not need a completed lesson, a separate form, or any coding skills.

Your design is a suggestion. Final design choices are made in collaboration with the SPOKES Coordinator. Some parts of your design may not appear in the final lesson. BeSpoke shows this note on Pick a look and again on Send. Before you send, your team ticks a box on Send to say you have read and understand this note.

Open BeSpoke
Using a local review preview? Save test design and Open test design use this computer’s test service. They do not share work online or send anything to Britt. The team-link, shared-save, and review-receipt instructions below apply to the connected team service.

Start with your team code

Britt puts your lesson’s 8-character team code in your team’s OneDrive folder. Choose Open team design, pick your lesson and type the code. It works on any computer. Keep it within your team: anyone with the code can change the team’s design.

Your team also has a team view link, pinned in your team’s private Teams channel. It opens your team’s latest saved design read-only, so anyone on the team can look at it without the code.

You do not choose a password or create an account. BeSpoke remembers the team on this browser when it can. On another computer, or if BeSpoke forgets, type the code from the OneDrive folder again. Lost it? Ask Britt for a new code.

At the start of a meeting

  1. Choose one person to build. They edit BeSpoke and share that browser window in Teams.
  2. Open the private team link. Check the lesson name and the last shared save. BeSpoke opens the latest saved design when there is no unsaved browser work to protect.
  3. If BeSpoke finds unsaved work, pause and read the message. Use Download backup before replacing work you may want to keep. Then choose whether to load the latest shared design or keep working on the browser draft.

Choose your look in four stages

  1. Lesson & team. BeSpoke opens here. Enter your team code first; you cannot move on until BeSpoke accepts it. Then choose your lesson, name your team and enter the spokesperson's name and email. If someone else will make the edits, add their name under Who is building the design? The stage stays marked until your team is identified. One spokesperson saves the team's decisions.
  2. Pick a look. Twelve looks sit in a strip under a large stage, grouped by mood: Calm, Bold, Bright and Classic. Each one shows your lesson's name. Point at a look to see it on the stage without choosing it. With the keyboard, Tab reaches the strip and the arrow keys move from look to look, showing each one on the stage. Click it to apply it; Undo takes it back. When the team agrees, choose Make it ours. Build my own starts from the plain builder design instead.
  3. Make it yours. Click any part of the slide to edit it. Its options appear in the toolbar just above the slide, and More options opens the Slide options drawer for the rest. You can click text, the navigation sidebar, boxes, buttons, the logo, the accent rule, the video frame, the activity panel or the watermark. For example, the navigation sidebar offers its own background color, text color and font. Click visible text and edit it in the Sample text field. Choose its color, size, horizontal alignment and Position within the text area. Add text adds an extra editable area. Select the background for Solid with one color or Gradient with start and end colors. The preset supplies the direction. A color you pick applies to every slide that shares it, so the Title and Chapter divider backgrounds match. The line under the toolbar names the other slides a color change reaches, and their thumbnails get a small changed tag. To keep a color on one slide, choose Change only this slide… on that line. It opens Slide options at the Only this slide switch, and you turn the switch on there. Undo stays beside the stages. Other slide types and More options remain available when you need them. To close Slide options, choose Hide options or press Escape. The slide keeps the shape you saw on Pick a look with the drawer open or closed. On a small screen with the drawer open, it fits itself to the space left.
  4. Send. Your five slides appear on one sheet beside a short summary of the look, its fonts and your changes. Read the note about the SPOKES Coordinator and tick the box under it. Add anything Britt should know, then let the spokesperson choose Save for the team and Send to Britt. See every setting opens the full list for all five slide types, including custom exceptions to the shared theme and readability advice.

Returning to a design? Your saved design opens where you left it, and you do not need to pick a look again. To start from a different look, choose Swap starting look on Make it yours. If your design is not one of the twelve looks, it stays first in the strip as the Your design card. Picking another look keeps your sample words but replaces the visual choices. Undo takes it back.

Let BeSpoke guide you

If you would rather be asked one question at a time, choose Guide me at the right end of the mood row in Pick a look. The guide goes slide by slide: your starting look, the shared look (fonts, background detail, sidebar, accent and button colors), then the title slide, chapter divider, text boxes, video slide and activity.

  • Each question shows small pictures of the choices. Point at one to see it on the preview without choosing it. Click it to choose.
  • Color questions suggest the four colors that are easiest to read on the current background, and More colors shows the rest. A color that may be hard to read is marked, with how far it falls short. You can still keep it.
  • Press Next to keep what you have. Use Skip this slide type to move on and Exit guide to leave at any point. Continue guide beside Guide me in Pick a look brings you back to the same question.
  • After each slide type a recap lists your choices. Choose Change beside one to go back to that question. The text boxes recap also lets you try your own sample lines.
  • Undo takes you back to the question where you made the change.

The guide and the slide editors make the same design, so you can switch between them whenever you like.

Shared theme or a custom slide choice?

Shared theme is optional and stays available as you work. It groups the lesson’s shared colors, heading and body fonts, and background detail. It also supplies default Sidebar, Accent, and Button colors. Navigation and buttons inherit the body font until you customize them directly in the preview. Their own font and text color do not change slide text. Shared Content background also colors the lesson canvas and the area outside a Band divider.

Under Paint colors, choose an element, then check Apply color to. It starts with This slide: the swatch shows the preview’s effective color, and painting changes only that field on that slide, including an existing custom choice. Choose Shared default deliberately to change the default for slides that follow it. These shared defaults apply across the design; feature overrides keep their own choices. Click a customized feature and choose Use shared defaults to clear its overrides. Undo restores them. Changing a background color keeps its other gradient color, direction, background detail, text, fonts, and watermark.

Fonts & background detail has its own Apply fonts & background detail to choice, also starting with This slide. The fonts and selected background detail match the preview. Choose a font or background detail to change just that setting on the current slide. Background detail thumbnails use its colors, gradient, and saved detail size. Plain removes only the detail; choosing one again uses the same size. Select Shared default when you want to change a shared font or background detail. A font for hidden text stays saved until you show that text in the slide editor.

Background detail offers Plain, Color block, Edge rail and Frame. Each is a flat shape that stays in the slide's outer margin, away from the words. When you open a design saved before this standard, BeSpoke changes any removed background detail to Plain and any other font to the heading font or the body font, then lists each change in a notice. Review the notice before you save.

A field marked Shared follows its shared theme setting. A field marked Custom uses that slide type’s own choice and stays the same when you change the shared default. For example, a custom Title heading color stays in place when you change the shared heading color; slide headings still marked Shared follow the new color. A font set to the heading font or the body font follows that lesson font, so changing the lesson font changes it too.

To make a field follow the shared theme again, choose Use shared theme in that field. This changes only that field on that slide type. It keeps the slide’s other choices and the other slide designs.

All eleven SPOKES colors remain available for every text and background role, and you can choose the lesson's heading font and body font independently from twelve font families. Every design uses those two fonts: a slide's text, a watermark, the sidebar and the buttons each pick the heading font or the body font. Contrast advice explains when text may be harder to read; it does not block your choice or silently change it.

Use the preview to agree

Switch preview tabs to compare the five designs. Your edits accumulate across them. While customizing on a small screen, selecting a feature brings its controls below the preview into view. The sample navigation menu opens its sidebar and selects it for editing. You may change sample words to see how they fit; writing lesson content is a separate task.

On Send, one line says how many supported design choices you share with six existing lessons. See the comparison shows the matches, differences, and unknowns. It is advice, not a guarantee that your design is unique, and it does not compare other teams’ private designs.

Saving is automatic

With team access open and the shared service available, BeSpoke saves shortly after you move between stages, and after about 30 seconds without a change. Wait for Saved to the shared design automatically or a confirmed manual save before treating the changes as shared. Your team can then open that version on another computer.

You can still choose Save for the team at any time, for example before you end the call. If the browser warns that your latest changes are not shared when you try to close BeSpoke, stay on the page and check the save status. A failed or pending save is not a confirmed save; download a backup if necessary.

At the next meeting, use the original team link again. Open team design also reloads the shared design when you need it. If another person saved a newer version, BeSpoke will tell you rather than overwrite their work.

Send the agreed look to Britt

Check that the Team Leader’s name is filled in, save the design, then choose Send to Britt. A pop-up tells you the click worked. Sending can take a minute or two, so there is no need to click again. Choose OK to close the pop-up; sending carries on. While the request is being handled, Send shows Sending to Britt…. When Britt's receipt arrives, Send shows Sent to Britt with your slides. If you sent from another stage, a short Sent to Britt. message appears at the top of the window instead. Treat the design as delivered only after one of these appears. If it fails or remains pending, keep your saved design and follow the retry message.

Thought of one more thing after sending? Type it in Add a note for Britt on the Sent to Britt screen and choose Send note to Britt. BeSpoke saves the note with your design, then sends both again. Note sent. appears under the button when Britt's receipt arrives. The note goes into the same public review proposal as the design, so the advice under Before sending applies to it too.

Britt gives final approval to every design before it is used in a lesson. Until then, your design is a suggestion that is waiting for Britt's approval. Britt reviews the visual choices. Sending or receiving a design does not build or publish a lesson. When a lesson is built later, the team checks that its approved choices appear correctly.

Before sending: use work contact details and non-sensitive sample text. The review proposal is stored in a public repository and can be read by others. Your private team link protects editing access; it does not make the submitted proposal private.

Keep a backup when you need one

Download backup saves a copy on your computer, usually in Downloads. You can place that file in the team’s OneDrive folder. You do not need to open or edit the file yourself. In BeSpoke, choose Open backup to bring its choices back.

A backup contains the design choices, not your team code. Shared saving still needs the code from your OneDrive folder. Opening a backup does not share it with the team. Review it and choose Save for the team when you are ready. Automatic saving starts again after that.

If something goes wrong

  • No internet or saving unavailable: if the page is already open, keep it open and download a backup before leaving. Shared saving and sending need a connection. A page opened fresh offline may not load the design tools. Return through your team link once service is available.
  • Someone saved a newer version: download a backup of your changes. Load the latest shared design, discuss the differences, and make the changes the team still wants. Save again. Do not repeatedly try to replace a newer version with an older draft.
  • The previous look was better: open Previous versions to review recent saved looks. Follow the restore steps and choose Save for the team when the team agrees; a restored look is not saved automatically. Restoring is a new save, so later history is kept.
  • The team link no longer works: ask Britt for the current private link. Do not invent a new code or create a different team record.
  • The browser copy disappeared: reopen the original team link for the last confirmed shared save. Use your backup for changes that were never saved online.
  • A request is still processing: leave the design intact. BeSpoke checks for the receipt automatically; use Check review status if the connection was interrupted or you want to check again. If the request failed, follow the retry message. Only Sent to Britt confirms it arrived.

On a shared computer

Save first and wait for confirmation. Keep a backup if you have unsaved changes. Before leaving, choose Leave session and close the BeSpoke tabs. This removes remembered access and local draft recovery from that browser; it does not delete the team’s shared design. Remove any private links or backup files you downloaded to the shared computer after moving them to your team’s approved storage.

View links and team links are different

The team view link always shows your team’s latest saved design, read-only. Choose Refresh in its banner to see a newer save. It does not show the Team Leader’s email or the notes for the design review. Use the builder link to edit and save. Share each link only with its intended audience.

The builder edits the design during the call. Confirm the shared save before leaving. Wait for Sent to Britt before treating the design as delivered.
