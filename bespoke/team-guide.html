Adult education instructor teams

Choose your lesson look together

BeSpoke helps your team choose the look of five reusable slide types: Title, Chapter divider, Text boxes, Video, and Activity. Share the preview during a Teams call and agree on the design together. The words and slides are samples. You do not need a completed lesson, a separate form, or any coding skills.

Open BeSpoke
Using a local review preview? Save test design and Open test design use this computer’s test service. They do not share work online or send anything to Britt. The team-link, shared-save, and review-receipt instructions below apply to the connected team service.

Start with your team link

Britt provides one private link for your lesson. Pin that original link in your team’s private Teams channel. Open it to return to your shared design, including on another computer. Keep it within your team: anyone with that link can change the team’s design.

You do not choose a password or create an account. BeSpoke remembers the team on this browser when it can. It removes the access code from the address bar, so return through the original pinned link rather than copying the address bar later.

At the start of a meeting

  1. Choose one spokesperson to operate BeSpoke and share that browser window in Teams.
  2. Open the private team link. Check the lesson name and the last shared save. BeSpoke opens the latest saved design when there is no unsaved browser work to protect.
  3. If BeSpoke finds unsaved work, pause and read the message. Use Download backup before replacing work you may want to keep. Then choose whether to load the latest shared design or keep working on the browser draft.

Choose your look in three stages

  1. Start. Check your lesson and team details. Choose an editable preset or Build my own. Each starts with usable choices. If a preset already fits, go straight to Review & save; you can also visit just one slide editor for a small change.
  2. Slide designs. Open Title, Chapter divider, Text boxes, Video, or Activity directly. Every editor is optional. Change only what your team wants, using the preview to see your choices together. Next slide type is an aid, not a checklist you must finish.
  3. Review & save. Check the effective choices for all five slide types, including custom exceptions to the shared theme. Review readability advice and add any notes for Britt. Let the spokesperson save the agreed design.

Returning to a design? Continue editing or review the current look. You do not need to choose a preset again. Use Change starting look deliberately when you want to replace the visual choices. Applying another preset keeps sample words but replaces the look; read its confirmation and use Undo if needed.

Shared theme or a custom slide choice?

Shared theme is optional and stays available as you work. It groups the lesson’s shared colors, heading and body fonts, and background texture. It also controls Sidebar, Accent, and Buttons, which do not have separate color controls in the slide editors. The shared body font supplies navigation and button text. Shared Content background also colors the lesson canvas and the area outside a Band divider.

A field marked Shared follows its shared theme setting. A field marked Custom uses that slide type’s own choice and stays the same when you change the shared default. For example, a custom Title heading font stays in place when you change the shared heading font; slide headings still marked Shared follow the new font.

To make a field follow the shared theme again, choose Use shared theme in that field. This changes only that field on that slide type. It keeps the slide’s other choices and the other slide designs.

All eleven SPOKES colors remain available for every text and background role, and you can choose heading and body fonts independently from twelve font families. Contrast advice explains when text may be harder to read; it does not block your choice or silently change it.

Use the preview to agree

Switch preview tabs to compare the five designs. Your edits accumulate across them. On a small screen, switch between Design and Preview. You may change sample words to see how they fit; writing lesson content is a separate task.

The similarity meter compares supported design choices with six existing lessons. Its details show matches, differences, and unknowns. It is advice, not a guarantee that your design is unique, and it does not compare other teams’ private designs.

Saving is automatic

With team access open and the shared service available, BeSpoke saves shortly after you move between stages, and after about 30 seconds without a change. Wait for Saved to the shared design automatically or a confirmed manual save before treating the changes as shared. Your team can then open that version on another computer.

You can still choose Save shared design at any time, for example before you end the call. If the browser warns that your latest changes are not shared when you try to close BeSpoke, stay on the page and check the save status. A failed or pending save is not a confirmed save; download a backup if necessary.

At the next meeting, use the original team link again. Open team design also reloads the shared design when you need it. If another person saved a newer version, BeSpoke will tell you rather than overwrite their work.

Send the agreed look to Britt

Check that the spokesperson’s name is filled in, save the design, then choose Send to Britt. While the request is being handled, BeSpoke shows processing. Wait for Britt received the review request. and the review receipt before treating it as delivered. If it fails or remains pending, keep your saved design and follow the retry message.

Britt reviews the visual choices. Sending or receiving a design does not build or publish a lesson. When a lesson is built later, the team checks that its approved choices appear correctly.

Before sending: use work contact details and non-sensitive sample text. The review proposal is stored in a public repository and can be read by others. Your private team link protects editing access; it does not make the submitted proposal private.

Keep a backup when you need one

Download backup saves a copy on your computer, usually in Downloads. You can place that file in the team’s OneDrive folder. You do not need to open or edit the file yourself. In BeSpoke, choose Open backup to bring its choices back.

A backup contains the design choices, not the private team access link. Keep the original pinned link for access to shared saving. Opening a backup does not share it with the team. Review it and choose Save shared design when you are ready. Automatic saving starts again after that.

If something goes wrong

  • No internet or saving unavailable: if the page is already open, keep it open and download a backup before leaving. Shared saving and sending need a connection. A page opened fresh offline may not load the design tools. Return through your team link once service is available.
  • Someone saved a newer version: download a backup of your changes. Load the latest shared design, discuss the differences, and make the changes the team still wants. Save again. Do not repeatedly try to replace a newer version with an older draft.
  • The previous look was better: open Previous versions to review recent saved looks. Follow the restore steps and choose Save shared design when the team agrees; a restored look is not saved automatically. Restoring is a new save, so later history is kept.
  • The team link no longer works: ask Britt for the current private link. Do not invent a new code or create a different team record.
  • The browser copy disappeared: reopen the original team link for the last confirmed shared save. Use your backup for changes that were never saved online.
  • A request is still processing: leave the design intact. BeSpoke checks for the receipt automatically; use Check review status if the connection was interrupted or you want to check again. If the request failed, follow the retry message. Only Britt received the review request. confirms it arrived.

On a shared computer

Save first and wait for confirmation. Keep a backup if you have unsaved changes. Before leaving, choose Leave session and close the BeSpoke tabs. This removes remembered access and local draft recovery from that browser; it does not delete the team’s shared design. Remove any private links or backup files you downloaded to the shared computer after moving them to your team’s approved storage.

View links and team links are different

An optional view link shows a read-only snapshot for discussion. It does not follow later changes and can include sample text and team details. Use your original private team link to edit and save. Share either kind of link only with its intended audience.

One spokesperson operates the design during the call. Confirm the shared save before leaving. Wait for the message confirming Britt received your review request.
