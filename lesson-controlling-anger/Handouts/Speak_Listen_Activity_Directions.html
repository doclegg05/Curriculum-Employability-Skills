Controlling Anger · Activity Directions

Speak/Listen Communication

A structured way to talk through a disagreement without it escalating.

Caution

Do not start this activity if both parties are in a state of extreme anger and irrational behavior. If either of you is too angry, separate for 10–20 minutes to cool down first. There should be no yelling — if yelling happens, stop and take more time to cool down.

1

Designate roles

Decide who is the Speaker and who is the Listener. You can pass an object back and forth to mark whose turn it is to speak, if that helps.

2

The Speaker

Speak slowly so the Listener has time to process. Pause after each point so the Listener can repeat your words back. If they repeat something you didn't say or mean, restate or clarify. Do not speak for longer than 5 minutes at a time — you'll get more turns.

3

The Listener

Actively listen — focus on nothing else except what the Speaker is saying. When they pause, paraphrase back what you heard in your own words, so the Speaker can tell whether you understood. Keep doing this until the Speaker is finished.

4

Switch!

When the Speaker is done, trade roles. Do not stay in one role longer than 5 minutes at a time — each person needs turns as both Speaker and Listener. It gets easier with practice.
