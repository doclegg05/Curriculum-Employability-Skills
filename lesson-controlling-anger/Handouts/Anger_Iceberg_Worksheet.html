The Anger Iceberg

Look beneath a moment of anger to find what's really driving it.

Instructions

The Anger Iceberg is the idea that anger is often just the visible part of a deeper emotional experience — like the tip of an iceberg, while other emotions and causes lie beneath the surface.

1

Above the waterline

In the tip of the iceberg (above the line), write "Anger" — the visible emotion you experience.

2

Below the waterline

In the larger portion below the line, list the underlying emotions, thoughts, and beliefs that contribute to your anger — frustration, fear, hurt, sadness, or disappointment.

3

Fill it in

Think of a specific situation that triggered your anger. What thoughts or beliefs did you hold about it? Be honest and open — this builds self-awareness.
