Why Goals Matter

Introduction

What Is a Goal?

A goal is a specific result you plan to reach by a certain time.

→Gives your effort direction
→Helps you make daily choices
→Shows your progress
→Builds confidence as you succeed
🎯

Clear goals turn effort into progress.

Goal Setting

Introduction

Short-Term vs. Long-Term Goals

Short-term goals are the steps that lead to long-term goals.

Short-Term
→Days to months
→Pass one GED subject test
→Open a savings account
→Update your résumé
Long-Term
→One year or more
→Earn a certificate
→Get a full-time job
→Buy a reliable car

Presentation 1

Vague vs. SMART

The same goal, made clear.

Vague
→I want a better job.
→I'll save money.
→I'll get in shape.
SMART
→I'll apply to 5 warehouse jobs by Friday.
→I'll save $25 every payday until June.
→I'll walk 20 minutes, 4 days a week.

Exit Ticket · Question 2 of 3

AGet healthier
BSave more money

✅ C. Save $20 a week for 10 weeks  It is specific, measurable, and has a time frame.
