Group Activity Scenarios

1 Scenario

What could you do?

2 Scenario

What could you do?

3 Scenario

What could you do?

4 Scenario

Your paycheck is six hours short. The next payday is two weeks away, and your rent is due Friday.

What could you do?

5 Scenario

A classmate asks to copy your homework because they ran out of time.

What could you do?

6 Scenario

You are offered two jobs. One pays more but has night shifts. The other pays less but fits your family's schedule. You must answer both by tomorrow.

What could you do?

7 Scenario

You have asked your landlord three times to fix a leaking sink. Now the water is ruining the cabinet underneath.

What could you do?

8 Scenario

What could you do?

9 Scenario

Your caseworker needs a signed form by Friday, but the office that signs it is closed until Monday.

What could you do?

10 Scenario

One member of your team project has not done their part, and the deadline is in two days.

What could you do?
