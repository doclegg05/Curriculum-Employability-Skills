Lunar Survival Activity

For the instructor

Goal
Students decide which supplies matter most when lives depend on it, first alone and then as a group.
Time
20 to 30 minutes. No background knowledge needed.
Before class
Print page 2, one copy per student, or one per pair or small group.

Steps

  1. Read the scenario aloud.
  2. Give students 5 to 10 minutes to rank the items.
  3. Read the NASA rankings from the answer sheet. Students write them in the NASA Ranking column.
  4. To score, find how far each of the student's ranks is from NASA's rank, ignoring plus or minus. Add up the differences. The lower the total, the better.

Score bands

0-25Excellent
26-32Good
33-45Average
46-55Fair
56-70Poor
71-112Very Poor

Lunar Survival Activity

Your space crew was supposed to land beside the mothership on the moon. A mechanical failure forced your ship down 200 miles away, and the landing damaged most of the equipment on board. The 15 items below came through undamaged. Rank them by how important they are for your crew's 200-mile trip back to the mothership. Number 1 is the most important and 15 the least. The list is in no particular order.
