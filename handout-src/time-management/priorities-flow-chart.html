Get Your Priorities Straight for the Day!

1

What do I HAVE to get done today?

(This could include going to work, so you don’t lose your job, taking the kids to school, going to a doctor’s appointment, etc.)

2

What would I LIKE to get done today?

(This could include laundry, exercise, making your family’s favorite meal.)

4

If I have time leftover, what could I spend that time doing?

(This could include self-care, relaxation, reading a book, watching TV, or even going to bed early.)

3

What could I do today that would make my week flow smoother or save me time later?

(This could include a quick evening tidying of the house, packing lunches for tomorrow, setting the coffee maker up for the morning.)
