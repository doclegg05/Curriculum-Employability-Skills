Shine Bright with STAR Interviews!

During an interview, you should be prepared to share examples from your academic, internship, or work experience. Behavioral interview questions often require examples, as employers believe past performance predicts future success. Use the S.T.A.R. method to structure your responses:

SSituation:Describe the situation and provide context.
TTask:Explain the task you needed to accomplish.
AAction:Describe the actions you took.
RResult:Share the outcome and what you learned.
