3 Minute Talk Activity

Warm Up

  1. Ask for a volunteer (no explanation).
  2. Have volunteer to come to the front of the room.
  3. Tell him/her that he/she must talk non-stop for 3 minutes (classroom appropriate).
  4. Instruct everyone else to sit quietly without interrupting the speaker.

After 3 minutes, discuss the activity.

  • Was it hard to talk for 3 minutes without feedback?(yes)
  • Was it hard to not speak and say what came to mind for 3 minutes?(yes)
  • Were we communicating with each other?(no)

Introduction

Have you ever known someone who was really hard to communicate with?

Teacher gives real-life examples:

  • Someone who thinks they are always right
  • Someone who never listens
  • Someone who never tries to see the other person's point of view

Learning how to effectively communicate with others can build your self-confidence and help you to achieve your personal and professional goals.
