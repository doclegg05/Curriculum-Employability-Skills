What Is Self-Advocacy?

Self-advocacy means standing up for yourself and your family by knowing what you need and asking for it.

Speak up for yourself

Say what you need, clearly and calmly.

Know your rights

At work, at school and with benefits.

Get the facts

Find out how things work before you decide.

Build your support team

People and programs who have your back.

Solve problems

Look for options, pick one, try it.

Ask for help early

Before a small problem becomes a big one.
