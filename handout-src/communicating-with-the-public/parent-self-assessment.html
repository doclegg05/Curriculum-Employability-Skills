Self-Assessment: Self-Advocacy for Job-Seeking Parents

This self-assessment helps you evaluate your ability to advocate for yourself, your child, your health, and your mental well-being while balancing job seeking and parenting. Answer each question honestly using the following scale:

Always (3 points) Sometimes (2 points) Rarely/Never (1 point)

Instructions:

  • Read each statement and mark the score that best describes you.
  • At the end of each section, total your points in the ‘Section Score’ box.
  • Add up all section scores to get your final score.
