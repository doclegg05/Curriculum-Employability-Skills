Six Steps to Asking for Help

Start
1

Name the problem.

What is going on? Write it in one sentence.

2

Know why you need help.

What is getting in your way?

3

Find who can help.

A supervisor, teacher, caseworker, family member or community group. List two.

4

Ask early.

Don’t wait until it’s a crisis. When will you ask?

5

Ask calmly and clearly.

Say what you need, be polite and stay open to answers.

6

Follow up.

If the first try doesn’t work, ask again or try someone else.
