Project Title: My Self-Advocacy, Workplace Readiness, & Family Advocacy Plan

Objective: Students will develop a personalized plan to enhance their self-advocacy skills while preparing for success in the workplace, job searching, balancing family responsibilities, and advocating for their children and families in schools and the community.

Steps:

  1. Self-Reflection Journal

    • Write about a challenge they face balancing home life, job responsibilities, job searching, and family needs.
    • Identify specific areas where self-advocacy could help, such as requesting flexible work arrangements, negotiating salary, accessing workplace benefits, or advocating for their children’s education and well-being.

  2. Research & Resources

    • Find at least three resources (job search assistance programs, workplace policies, school programs, community services, or parent support groups).
    • Summarize how each resource can support workplace readiness, career success, and family well-being.

  3. Communication Strategy

    • Draft an email or script for a conversation with an employer, hiring manager, teacher, school administrator, or community organization to request support, accommodations, or services.
    • Peer review and practice delivering the request.

  4. Job Search & Workplace Readiness

    • Identify job search challenges (e.g., flexible work schedules, childcare needs, skill development).
    • Develop a strategy for advocating for their needs in resumes, cover letters, and interviews.
    • Research companies that support working parents and review employee benefits such as childcare assistance or remote work options.
    • Learn about professional etiquette, workplace expectations, and strategies for career advancement.

  5. Advocating for Children & Family in Schools & the Community

    • Identify challenges their children face in school (e.g., learning support, special education, extracurricular access).
    • Learn how to communicate effectively with teachers and school administrators.
    • Explore ways to get involved in community programs that support parents and families.
    • Research laws and policies that protect parents’ and children’s rights in education and community resources.

  6. Advocacy Action Plan

    • Outline 3-5 concrete steps they will take in the next month to improve their self-advocacy, workplace readiness, and family advocacy.

  7. Presentation or Reflection

    • Share key takeaways in a brief written reflection or informal discussion.
    • Discuss how self-advocacy in work, school, and community settings will impact their personal and professional growth.
