Connecting or Disconnecting?

How to play (2 or 3 players): Cut the cards apart and stack them face down.

  1. Player 1 draws a card and reads it out loud.
  2. Player 2 says whether it connects (brings people together) or disconnects (pushes them apart).
  3. If Player 2 is right, they keep the card. If wrong, it goes to the bottom of the stack.
  4. Switch roles. Play until the stack is gone.
