Getting Ready to Speak Up At Work or School

Speaking up goes better when you plan what to say.

The situation

Who do I need to talk to?
What do I need from them?
When and where will we talk?

Plan what to say

  1. What is working well right now?

  2. What is not working?

  3. What have I already tried?

  4. What would make the biggest difference?

  5. What am I willing to do in return?

  6. What will I do if the answer is no?

Getting Ready to Speak Up For a Job Interview

Speaking up goes better when you plan what to say.

The situation

What job am I interviewing for?
Who will I meet?
What do I know about the company?

Plan what to say

  1. What skills make me a good fit?

  2. What do I need from this job (pay, hours, schedule)?

  3. What questions will I ask them?

  4. What concern might they ask about, and how will I answer?

  5. What would make me turn down an offer?

  6. What do I want them to remember about me?

Getting Ready to Speak Up When Negotiating

Speaking up goes better when you plan what to say.

The situation

What am I asking for (pay, schedule, duties)?
Who decides?
What do they care about?

Plan what to say

  1. What is fair pay for this job in my area?

  2. What is my best result?

  3. What is the least I can accept?

  4. What can I be flexible on?

  5. What proof shows I deserve this?

  6. What will I say first?
