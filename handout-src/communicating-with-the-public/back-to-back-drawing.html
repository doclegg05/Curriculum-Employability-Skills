Back-to-Back Drawing

Directions: Pairs sit back to back. One person is the Describer and holds a picture card. The other is the Drawer and has blank paper and a pencil.

Describer

Holds the picture card and explains it in words.

Drawer

Has blank paper and a pencil, and draws what they hear.

How it works

  1. Round 1The Describer explains the picture so the Drawer can copy it. No peeking, and no showing.
  2. The Drawer stays silent the whole time: no questions, no nods, no “what?”
  3. After 5 to 10 minutes, compare the drawing with the card.
  4. Round 2Switch roles and use a new card. This time the Drawer may ask questions. Compare again.

Talk it over

Describer

  • What did you do to make your directions clear?
  • How did it feel to get no response?
  • What would you say differently next time?

Drawer

  • Which directions were easy to follow, and which were confusing?
  • How did round 2 change when you could ask questions?
  • What does this tell you about listening at work?
