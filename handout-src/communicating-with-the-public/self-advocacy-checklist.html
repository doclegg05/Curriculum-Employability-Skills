Self-Advocacy & Self-Determination Checklist

1Knowing Yourself & Your Needs

  • I know my strengths and what I am good at.
  • I know what kind of job I want and what works for my family.
  • I understand the problems I might face (childcare, transportation, job skills).
  • I know my rights as a worker (fair pay, time off, workplace rules).
  • I can speak up for what I need at work.
  • I take care of my health and well-being while looking for a job.

2Job Search & Getting Ready for Work

  • I know where to look for jobs.
  • I have a resume that shows my skills and experience.
  • I can talk about why I would be good for a job.
  • I practice answering interview questions.
  • I am ready to explain work gaps or changes in my career.
  • I know how to ask for better pay or hours.
  • I have a way to look professional online (LinkedIn or social media).
  • I know where to get help with job searching (job centers, career programs).

3Balancing Work & Family

  • I look for jobs that fit my family’s needs (flexible hours, remote work).
  • I have a plan for childcare when I start working.
  • I know my rights for family leave and sick time.
  • I know how to manage stress and balance work with family.
  • I set clear work and home boundaries.
  • I have a backup plan if my child gets sick or something unexpected happens.
  • I talk with my family about my job plans.

4Making Good Decisions

  • I make choices that help me and my family.
  • I know where to go for help when I have a problem.
  • I can ask for fair pay and good working conditions.
  • I am comfortable talking about my schedule, pay, or job duties.
  • I adjust my job search if things are not working.
  • I feel confident making decisions, even when it’s hard.

5Finding Support & Connections

  • I have people I can ask for help and advice.
  • I stay connected with job groups or community programs.
  • I feel comfortable asking others about job openings.
  • I reach out to old coworkers, bosses, or friends for job leads.
  • I try to meet new people at job fairs or events.
  • I talk to other parents about balancing work and family.

6Knowing My Rights & Money Management

  • I know my rights for fair pay, time off, and workplace rules.
  • I have a plan to manage my money while looking for a job.
  • I know about programs that help parents with money (childcare, food, housing).
  • I understand what comes with a job (pay, healthcare, vacation time).
  • I know about taxes and other costs that come with working.
  • I know how to report unfair treatment at work.

7Learning & Growing

  • I am open to learning new skills.
  • I ask for feedback to improve my job search.
  • I set small goals to keep moving forward.
  • I use free classes or training programs to build new skills.
  • I stay updated on job trends and opportunities.
  • I work on improving my speaking and communication skills.
  • I take care of myself and celebrate my progress.
