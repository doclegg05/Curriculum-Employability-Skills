Role-Play Activity: Practicing Healthy Anger

Purpose: This activity helps participants practice expressing healthy, assertive anger.

Scenario 1

Overlooked Input in a Meeting

  • Person A: Feels their idea was used without credit.
  • Person B: Coworker who presented the idea.

Goal: Express feelings clearly and work toward resolution.

Scenario 2

Repeated Missed Deadlines

  • Person A: Impacted by delays and feeling frustrated.
  • Person B: Struggling with deadlines.

Goal: Communicate frustration constructively.

Instructions:

  1. Participants read their roles.
  2. Person A expresses healthy anger (calm, assertive, specific).
  3. Person B responds respectfully.
  4. Continue for 3–5 minutes.
  5. Switch roles if time allows.

Healthy Anger Prompts:

  • 'I felt ___ when ___ happened.'
  • 'I want to resolve this so it doesn't escalate.'
  • 'Here's what I need going forward...'
  • 'Can we find a solution together?'

Debrief Questions:

  1. How did it feel to use healthy anger?
  2. What made the conversation productive?
  3. Did the anger fade once you focused on solutions?
  4. How might this translate to real workplace situations?
