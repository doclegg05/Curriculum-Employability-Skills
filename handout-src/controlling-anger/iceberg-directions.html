Anger Iceberg Directions

Anger is often only the part of an iceberg that shows above the water. Underneath sit the feelings, thoughts and beliefs that feed it. This activity helps you find what sits below your anger.

  1. 1

    Think of a time you got angry. Write a few words about it at the top of your iceberg page.

  2. 2

    Above the waterline, write "Anger" and how it showed: what you said, what you did, or what you felt in your body.

  3. 3

    Below the waterline, write the feelings underneath. Common ones are fear, hurt, frustration, shame and grief.

    FearHurtFrustrationShameGrief
  4. 4

    Add the thoughts or beliefs that came with those feelings. For example, someone cuts you off in traffic. Above the water is anger. Below it might be fear of a crash, or the thought "They don't respect me."

  5. 5

    Look at what you wrote below the line. Which feeling could you talk about directly next time?
