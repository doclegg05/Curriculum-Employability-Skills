The Four Styles

Avoidance

Withdrawing, staying quiet, avoiding conflict to keep the peace

It helps
Gives everyone time to cool down.
Watch out for
Problems that never get solved, and feelings that build up.
Best used when
Things are too heated to talk right now, or the issue is small.

Forceful

Intimidation, overpowering, expressing emotions without filter

It helps
Gets fast action, but through fear.
Watch out for
Damaged trust, and people who stop speaking up around you.
Best used when
Never as a threat. In an emergency, a short, firm instruction such as “Stop. Step back.” is enough.

Collaborative

Hearing all sides, pursuing resolution constructively, maintaining relationships

It helps
Solves the problem and keeps the relationship.
Watch out for
It takes time, and both people have to be willing.
Best used when
The relationship and the result both matter.

Indirect Control

Manipulation, maneuvering situations, appearing collaborative but controlling

It helps
Can keep tension from boiling over in the moment.
Watch out for
People feel used once they notice, and trust drops.
Best used when
Rarely. Say what you want openly instead.
