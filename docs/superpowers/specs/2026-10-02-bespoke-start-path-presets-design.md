# BeSpoke start flow, step path and presets

Status: PR 2 (flow) superseded by `2026-10-06-bespoke-quick-pick-design.md`. PR 1 carries forward. Approved by Britt, 2026-10-02, with the color steps reordered so sidebar color and sidebar text color are steps 5 and 6. Extended the same day with the intake wizard, the builder name and the team view link. Nothing here is built. Baseline is `origin/main` at `13a808d`, the preview editor merge.

## Problem

A Team Leader who opens BeSpoke meets the whole Start stage at once: the team form, Guide me, Build my own and six presets on one page. Britt's verdict on 2026-10-02 was that the options are right, and the page shows too many of them together, which makes choosing hard.

The six presets also look alike. All six lead with navy, royal or mauve. All six use three boxes across, the stacked video layout and the callout activity layout. Green and gold never lead.

Guide me already asks one question per screen, but it runs slide by slide through about 40 questions. Britt wants a short path in a different order: colors, then layout, then fonts.

Each team has one private link today, and it grants edit rights to whoever holds it. Nothing says who builds, and a teammate who misses the call has no way to see the design without that edit link.

## Solution

A short intake wizard, a twelve-step path for teams who start from scratch, and nine presets that differ from each other. The preview stays clickable throughout. One person builds, and everyone else opens a read-only team view link.

1. 'Intake wizard' - Start walks four short screens, one question each: the lesson team, the Team Leader, who is building, and how the team works.
2. 'Scratch or preset' - the next screen offers one choice, Start from scratch or Start from a preset.
3. 'Step by step' - from scratch walks twelve steps, one decision per screen: primary color, secondary color, header text color, body text color, sidebar color, sidebar text color, the layout of each of the five slide types, then fonts.
4. 'All eleven colors' - every color screen shows all eleven brand colors. No step limits a later step.
5. 'Jump anywhere' - a step strip opens any step in any order.
6. 'Preview clicks' - clicking an element on the preview during the path opens the matching step, or the preview editor when no step matches.
7. 'Sidebar text color' - the sidebar text color becomes a choice. It starts on Automatic.
8. 'Nine presets' - Open Air, Gold Standard and Slate join the six existing names, and every preset is redesigned.
9. 'Preset gallery' - each preset shows two small rendered slides in place of the "Aa" chip.
10. 'One builder' - the saved design names the Team Leader and, when different, the one person who builds.
11. 'Team view link' - each team gets a second link that opens its latest saved design read-only.

### Start

The three stages stay: Start, Slide designs, Review & save. Start becomes a short sequence of screens.

**Intake wizard.** Four screens, each with one question, a "Getting started · n of 4" counter, and Back and Continue.

1. **Your lesson team.** A dropdown of the six Round 2 lessons. A builder link selects it and locks it, as today. The screen then asks the leader to confirm.
2. **Team Leader.** The Team Leader's name. Required. It saves to `team.spokesperson.name`. Continue is disabled until the name has text.
3. **Who is building the design?** Two choices: the Team Leader, or someone else on the team. Someone else needs a name before Continue enables. It saves to `team.builder.name`. The Team Leader is the default and saves no builder field.
4. **How your team works.** Four short lines and no fields: one person builds; the team decides together on a call while the builder shares their screen; everyone else opens the team view link any time; the builder link stays with the builder and the Team Leader.

The wizard has no Team name or email field. A saved design that already has them keeps them. A new design saves an empty team name and no email. A proposal that prints the team name falls back to the Team Leader's name when the saved team name is empty, as `scripts/bespoke-write-submission.py` does today. The builder says Team Leader wherever it said Spokesperson.

A finished intake is remembered in the browser draft and is not asked again after a reload.

**Screen 5, How do you want to start?** Two large cards. Start from scratch opens the step path on the default design. Start from a preset opens the preset gallery.

**Returning teams.** When a meaningful design exists, Start skips the wizard and shows the Team Leader, the lesson team and the builder, with Continue editing, Step by step, Review & save, Start over and Edit team. Start over opens Screen 5. Edit team reopens the wizard and returns here. Replacing an existing design asks for confirmation first, keeps the sample words and can be undone, as today.

**View-only sessions** skip the wizard and see the returning view without Step by step, Start over or Edit team.

### Step path

The path replaces the Guide me question list and reuses the engine in `GUIDE_DIR`. The builder calls it Step by step. The slide-by-slide questions are removed, not kept as a second path.

| # | Screen | Sets | Preview shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary color | `roles.titleBackground`, `roles.dividerBackground` | Title slide |
| 2 | Secondary color | `roles.titleBackgroundEnd`, `roles.accent`, `roles.button` | Title slide |
| 3 | Header text color | `roles.heading` | Text boxes |
| 4 | Body text color | `roles.body` | Text boxes |
| 5 | Sidebar color | `roles.sidebar` | Text boxes |
| 6 | Sidebar text color | `roles.sidebarText` | Text boxes |
| 7 | Title slide layout | `slides.title.layout` | Title slide |
| 8 | Chapter divider layout | `slides.divider.layout` | Chapter divider |
| 9 | Text boxes layout | `slides.cards.layout` | Text boxes |
| 10 | Video slide layout | `slides.video.layout` | Video slide |
| 11 | Activity layout | `slides.activity.layout` | Activity |
| 12 | Fonts | `fonts.heading`, then `fonts.body` | Title slide, then Text boxes |

Rules for every screen:

- One question, one set of options, and the live preview beside it.
- A color screen shows all eleven swatches in catalog order, each with its name. Text color screens add the model's readability note to each swatch. The note is advice. No swatch is disabled.
- A layout screen shows each option as a small rendered slide.
- The font screens show all twelve fonts, each set in its own face. Heading font and body font are two screens under step 12.
- A pick applies at once and goes into Undo history.
- Back and Next are always present. Next without a pick moves on, so there is no separate Skip button.
- After the last screen a recap lists the twelve choices. It offers Review & save and Keep editing.

**Step strip.** A strip above the question names all twelve steps in three groups: Colors, Layout, Fonts. The leader can open any step at any time. The current step carries `aria-current="step"`. A step the leader has answered shows a check mark. At phone width the strip becomes "Step 4 of 12" with a menu that lists every step.

**Title slide text.** Title and divider text sit on the primary color, not on white. After the leader answers Primary or Secondary, the path sets `roles.titleText` and `roles.subtitle` to the readable ink for the new colors. The readable ink is White or Royal, whichever has the higher lowest contrast across the title slide's colors. The path changes each of those two roles only while it still holds the readable ink for the previous colors. A color the leader chose by hand stays.

**Slide-level choices stay.** The steps set shared colors and fonts. A slide type that has its own Custom value keeps it, and the screen shows the existing Shared and Custom scope note.

**Text boxes layout.** The three arrangements only look different once the number of boxes is set. Screen 9 carries a small "Number of boxes" control under the arrangement options. This is the one screen with a second control.

**Saved place.** The path's position saves in the browser draft, as Guide me's does. A draft that holds an old Guide me position opens with the design intact and no path in progress.

### Preview clicks during the path

This replaces the 2026-10-01 rule that Guide me owns the preview.

| Click on | Result |
|---|---|
| Title or divider background | Step 1, Primary color |
| A content slide heading | Step 3, Header text color |
| Content slide body text | Step 4, Body text color |
| Sidebar background | Step 5, Sidebar color |
| Sidebar text | Step 6, Sidebar text color |
| A text box | Step 9, Text boxes layout |
| Anything else | The preview editor opens on that element |

When the preview editor opens from the path, the toolbar shows a button, "Back to step N", with the step's name. The path keeps its place. Keyboard selection follows the same table. Outside the path the preview editor works as it does today, with the sidebar added as a selectable element.

### Sidebar text color

- New optional field `design.roles.sidebarText`. Its value is one of the eleven color ids. An absent field means Automatic, which is today's `inkFor` choice. Choosing Automatic removes the field.
- The catalog lists it under a separate `optionalRoles` key, so the eleven required roles and the code that loops over them stay untouched. The schema generator leaves it out of `required`. `structuralErrors` accepts a design with or without it.
- `cssForDesign` sets `--role-sidebar-ink` from the field when it is present.
- `contrastIssues` adds one advisory, sidebar text on the sidebar color at 4.5 to 1, when the field is present.
- Shared theme gains a Sidebar text control. The preview editor gains a Sidebar element with Sidebar color and Sidebar text color fields. Its scope note says the sidebar is shared by every content slide.
- The generated CSS and the build contract carry the chosen color through the shared model. `docs/bespoke/builder-handoff.md` documents the field.
- Existing saved designs have no field and look the same.

### Roles and links

One person edits. This is enforced by which link a person holds, not by who they are. There are no accounts.

| Link | Who gets it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Builder link, `#team=<lesson>.<code>` | The Team Leader, who passes it to the builder | Edit, save, send. This is today's team link, unchanged |
| Team view link, `#view=<lesson>.<code>` | Everyone on the team. Pinned in the team's channel | Opens the latest saved design, read-only |

- The service keeps a second table of code hashes, `BESPOKE_VIEW_KEYS`, one view code per lesson. It is optional: without it the service behaves as before.
- A view code may only open the latest saved design for its own lesson. Save, send, history, an older version and review status are refused with one plain message.
- The page removes the code from the address bar and keeps it for the browser tab, so a reload stays in the view.
- The view page never reads or writes the browser draft.
- A banner names the team, the mode, the last saved time and the builder, for example "Money Management team · View only · Saved Oct 2, 2:14 PM · Built by Dana". A Refresh button loads anything newer.
- Before the first save the view page says the team has not saved a design yet.
- `PROVISIONER` gains `--add-view-links`. It adds the six view links beside the existing private files and changes none of them. It never regenerates the team codes or the encryption key.
- The expected way to work is a Teams call with the builder sharing their screen. The view link covers teammates who miss the call or want the design on their own screen.
- If two people use the builder link at once, the existing stale-save check still refuses the older save.
- The access change gets a security review before the pull request.

Rules the presets must keep, each enforced by a test:

- Every preset is a valid design with no readability advisory.
- No two presets share the same title background and title text pair.

A design saved from an old preset keeps its own colors and layouts, because a saved design stores every choice. Only its `startingPoint` label points at a preset whose look has changed.

### Saved-design format and deploy order

Three things change the saved-design format:

1. The optional `roles.sidebarText` field.
2. Three new preset ids in the `startingPoint` list. The schema generator and `structuralErrors` both build that list from `CATALOG`.
3. The optional `team.builder` field, holding one `name` of 1 to 200 characters.

`SERVICE` bundles the schema, the catalog and the model, and rejects fields and ids it does not know. The service is redeployed before the builder change merges, as on 2026-10-01. The new service accepts every design the old one accepted. Claude stages the service and Britt runs the deploy.

The view codes add one Netlify setting, `BESPOKE_VIEW_KEYS`. Britt generates the six view links with `PROVISIONER`, sets that value, deploys, and pins one view link in each team's channel. `SETUP_GUIDE` carries the steps.

The format stays `bespoke-selection/v2`. There is no migration.
