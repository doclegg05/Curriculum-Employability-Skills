# BeSpoke Quick pick

Status: design approved by Britt in chat, 2026-10-06, revised the same day for preset variety and a Color scheme row, then revised on 2026-10-07 after Britt said Quick pick is advisory only and carries no readability gates. Nothing here is built yet. Baseline is `claude/bespoke-workflow-presets-fc20df` at `652f997`, which is `origin/main` (`13a808d`) plus the unmerged PR 1 of the 2026-10-02 spec.

This spec replaces the flow half (PR 2) of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-bespoke-start-path-presets-design.md`. That spec's PR 1 is built and carries forward here.

## Purpose

Quick pick gathers opinions. Teams who want a say in how the slides look pick a look and tweak it, and Britt reads what they send. It is not the final design. The final design comes from Britt and the administrator, with Claude Design, and may borrow from what teams suggest. So a combination a team picks can be ugly or hard to read, and that is fine. Quick pick gates nothing on readability or accessibility and shows no readability warnings. The existing full builder keeps its advisory notes unchanged.

## Problem

Britt's verdict on 2026-10-06: the builder is too complicated, and a team would take a long time to settle on a look. The Start stage, Slide designs, Guide me's 40 questions and the twelve-step path planned on 2026-10-02 all ask for many separate decisions. Teams need one choice and a few small changes, done in one short call.

## Solution

Teams pick one of twelve finished looks that differ as much as the catalog allows. Then on one screen they can borrow another look's color scheme and change four things: background, font, sidebar and text color. Nothing is gated on readability, because the result is a suggestion for Britt and not the final design. The full builder stays available behind a small More options link.

1. 'Who's here' - one screen that confirms the lesson and asks for the Team Leader's name.
2. 'Pick a look' - a gallery of twelve presets, each with two small rendered slides.
3. 'Make it yours' - a large live preview and five rows of options beside it.
4. 'Save and send' - the existing shared Save, reopen and Send to Britt.
5. 'More options' - a link that opens the existing builder with the design carried over.

## Variables

- `BUILDER_APP`: `bespoke/builder-app.mjs`. Stages, team form, drafts, save. 1,879 lines at baseline, over the 800-line limit, so new code goes in new files and this file only gains the hooks that call them.
- `BUILDER_MODEL`: `bespoke/builder-model.mjs`. Validation, `contrastIssues`, `inkFor`, CSS, slide rendering.
- `CATALOG`: `bespoke/builder-catalog.json`. Colors, fonts, `fontPairings`, presets.
- `PRESET_TABLE`: `scripts/build-bespoke-presets.mjs`. Generates the presets in `CATALOG` from one table.
- `QUICK_DIR`: `bespoke/quick/`. New. Holds the Quick pick option table, its pure functions and its screens.
- `SCHEMA_V2`: `bespoke/selection-v2.schema.json`, generated by `scripts/generate-selection-v2-schema.mjs`.
- `SERVICE`: `netlify/functions/bespoke-handoff.mjs`. Staged with `scripts/bespoke-stage-service.mjs`.
- `QUALITY`: `scripts/quality.sh`.

## Implementation notes

### Decisions from Britt, 2026-10-06

1. Teams see presets first and pick one.
2. After the pick they change four things only: background color, font, sidebar color and text color.
3. The detailed builder stays, hidden behind a More options link.
4. Background is one choice that sets both the title and chapter color and a matching light tint for content slides.
5. Text color sets headings and body together.
6. Font is a choice of ready-made pairs, a heading font with a matching body font.
7. Twelve presets that differ in colors, fonts, texture and layouts as much as the catalog allows. The nine from 2026-10-02 stay and three new ones fill the gaps. Britt can ask for a fuller redo after seeing all twelve rendered.
8. The changes sit on one screen, as rows beside the preview.
9. The flow does not ask who is building the design.
10. A Color scheme row lets a team take the colors of any of the twelve presets while keeping their chosen look's fonts, texture and layouts. The other color rows fine-tune from there.
11. Text color stays one choice for headings and body.
12. Quick pick is advisory. No readability or accessibility gate, test or warning applies to what a team can pick (2026-10-07).

### Screen 1, Who's here

- The private team link already selects and locks the lesson. The screen names the lesson and asks the Team Leader's name. The name is required and saves to `team.spokesperson.name`. Continue stays disabled until the name has text.
- No team name, email or builder question. A saved design that already holds them keeps them.
- A returning team, one whose shared or browser draft holds a meaningful design, skips this screen and Pick a look and opens Make it yours on that design.
- A team view link session skips Quick pick and shows the read-only view built in PR 1 of the 2026-10-02 spec.

### Screen 2, Pick a look

- Twelve cards. Each shows the rendered title slide and the rendered text boxes slide at thumbnail size, the preset's name and its one-line blurb.
- One click applies the preset through `applyPreset`, which keeps the sample words, and opens Make it yours.
- On a returning team, choosing a different look replaces the current design. The page asks first, and Undo brings the old design back.

### Screen 3, Make it yours

The preview shows the title slide and a content slide. Five rows sit beside it at laptop width and below it at phone width, in the order below. A pick applies at once, updates the preview and goes into Undo history.

**Color scheme** comes first. It shows the twelve presets' color schemes as small swatch strips with their names, and the look the team picked is marked. A pick sets every color role, including `sidebarText`, to that preset's values. It leaves fonts, texture, layouts and sample words alone. So a team can keep Slate's layouts and wear Harvest's colors.

The four rows below it each start with Keep scheme. It restores the chosen scheme's own values for the roles that row controls.

| Row | Options | Roles one pick sets |
|---|---|---|
| Color scheme | The twelve presets' colors | All eleven color roles and `sidebarText` |
| Background | Navy, Royal, Blue, Green, Mauve, Gold | `titleBackground`, `dividerBackground`, `titleBackgroundEnd`, `contentBackground`, `titleText`, `subtitle` |
| Font | Five pairs from `fontPairings` | `fonts.heading`, `fonts.body` |
| Sidebar | Navy, Royal, Gray, Green, Mauve | `sidebar`, and removes `sidebarText` so the text color is Automatic |
| Text color | Royal, Navy, Gray, Mauve | `heading`, `body` |

Background options, each a bold color, its blend partner and its content tint:

| Option | Title and divider | Blend partner | Content tint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navy | Navy | Blue | White |
| Royal | Royal | Navy | Mist |
| Blue | Blue | Navy | White |
| Green | Green | Gold | White |
| Mauve | Mauve | Royal | White |
| Gold | Gold | Green | Mist |

After a background pick, `titleText` and `subtitle` are set to White or Royal, whichever has the higher contrast on the bold color. This is a convenience so a first pick looks sensible. It is not a gate, and the Text color row can override it.

The five font pairs are DM Serif Display + Outfit, Playfair Display + Inter, Merriweather + Source Sans 3, Vollkorn + Fira Sans and Crimson Pro + Work Sans.

The lists are short so the rows stay quick to scan, not to prevent poor contrast. Silver is left out as a content tint and Blue as a text color only to keep the rows short, and a team can still reach them in More options.

The option table lives in `QUICK_DIR` as data, not in `CATALOG`, so `SERVICE` and `SCHEMA_V2` do not change for it.

**Selected state.** Quick pick keeps the chosen scheme in the browser draft. When it is missing, for example on another computer, the Color scheme row finds it from `accent` and `button`, which only that row sets. Where two presets share the pair, the first one in the preset list is shown as selected. That is fine, because the saved scheme in the draft is the real source and this fallback only matters on another computer. Each other row marks the option that matches the current design. A design changed in More options can match none of them. That row then shows Custom as selected, and any pick replaces it.

**Actions** under the rows: Save & send to Britt, Pick a different look, and a small More options link.

### More options

More options opens the existing Slide designs stage on the same design. Guide me, the preview editor and Review & save behave as they do at baseline. A Back to Quick pick link returns to Make it yours, where rows show Custom wherever the design no longer matches an option.

### Presets

Twelve presets: Professional, Modern, Serious, Light-hearted, Fun, Outspoken, Open Air, Gold Standard, Slate, Harvest, Bold Statement and Fresh Start. The first nine come from 2026-10-02 and stay as they are. The last three are new, in `PRESET_TABLE`.

The nine lean on a few choices. Four use the centered title slide, five put their text boxes in columns, three use a Royal sidebar, and three use the plain texture. The three new presets lean the other way.

The catalog sets the limits: eleven colors, twelve fonts, five textures, four title layouts, four divider layouts, three text box arrangements, three video layouts and four activity layouts. With twelve presets some choices must repeat. The goal is as much variety as the catalog allows, so a team sees twelve looks that feel different.

Britt reviews all twelve rendered presets before PR 1 merges, and the values can change in that review. Ugly is acceptable. Variety matters more than polish.

Variety checks, each a test:

- Every preset has its own heading font. Twelve presets, twelve fonts.
- No color is the sidebar of more than three presets. Three of the nine from 2026-10-02 already share Royal, and the new presets add none to it.
- Every pair of presets differs on at least 7 of the 13 compared fields already in `scripts/test-bespoke-builder-model.mjs`. The nine presets from 2026-10-02 already meet this bar, and the three new ones must too.

The three new presets, Harvest, Bold Statement and Fresh Start, are written by hand in `PRESET_TABLE` and aimed at the gaps the nine leave: layouts, textures and colors that few of them use. There is no search script.

Rules every preset keeps, also enforced by a test:

- It is a structurally valid design. The existing checks that a preset raises no contrast advisory (`scripts/test-bespoke-builder-model.mjs`, lines 28 to 49) keep covering the nine from 2026-10-02. The three new presets are exempt, because readability is not a gate here.
- No preset is an exact match for a released lesson.
- No preset carries per-slide custom styles (`roleStyles`), so the rows always reach the slides.

### Saved-design format and deploy order

The rows only write values a design already stores. The one format change is three new preset ids in the `startingPoint` list, which `SCHEMA_V2` and `structuralErrors` build from `CATALOG`.

`SERVICE` rejects ids it does not know, so it is redeployed before PR 1 merges, as on 2026-10-01. The new service accepts every design the old one accepted. Claude stages the service and Britt runs the deploy. The same deploy carries the 2026-10-02 PR 1 changes: `sidebarText`, `team.builder` and the team view codes in `BESPOKE_VIEW_KEYS`.

The format stays `bespoke-selection/v2`. There is no migration.

## Out of scope

- Readability or accessibility gates, tests or warnings in Quick pick.
- Changes to the full builder, Guide me or the preview editor beyond the More options and Back to Quick pick links.
- New colors, fonts, textures or layout options in `CATALOG`.
- Layout choices in Quick pick. A team that wants a different layout picks another preset or uses More options.
- Any change to the six released lessons or to lesson building.
- A hosted round trip with a real team link. It stays an open item.
