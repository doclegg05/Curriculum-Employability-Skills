## Day-one objectives

By the end of the first teach, a learner can:

1. Make a cash plan that separates cash from SNAP/EBT.
2. Use one budgeting tool for this week's money in and money out.
3. Name two in-store tactics (deals, light-touch).
4. Name one safe next step (PATH, DoHS, 211, or FRN) without oversharing.

**Britt starters (instructor-owned meaning):** couponing and deal shopping · budgeting tools · in-store tactics.

Do not write hooks, stories, or quotes for those starters. Wait for the intake.

## Out of scope / avoid

- Shame language ("welfare," "you should have known," deficit framing)
- Forced disclosure of benefit amounts, case numbers, or household composition
- Legal or eligibility advice
- "Working the system"
- Middle-class surplus budgets
- Stale SNAP or TANF dollar figures treated as current gospel
- Invented 211 scripts or FRN promises

Cite-or-abstain: if a figure, a form name, or an eligibility rule is not on the instructor intake, leave it off the slides.
