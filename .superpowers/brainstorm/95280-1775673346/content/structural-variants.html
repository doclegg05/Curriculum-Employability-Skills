How many structural variants should we build?

More variants = more visual variety but more template maintenance. Click your preference.

3

Three Variants

Pick 3 of the 4 shown above. Each variant gets ~6 lessons. Good balance of variety and maintainability.

4

All Four Variants

Use all 4 layouts. Each gets 4-5 lessons. Maximum structural variety. More to maintain.
