The Problem

Despite different fonts, all 6 lessons share: the same dark-blue sidebar, the same white content area, the same blue-to-dark title gradient, the same card layout pattern, and the same section divider style. Swap the titles and you can't tell them apart.

What Could Change?

Click the aspect that bothers you most about the sameness above.

A

Layout Structure

The sidebar, slide area, and navigation skeleton all look identical. Different lessons could have different spatial arrangements.

B

Color Personality

No visual "mood" per lesson. The anger lesson should feel different from the communication lesson through color emphasis, backgrounds, and tonal shifts.

C

Content Components

Every lesson uses the same cards-grid and takeaways in the same patterns. More variety in how information is presented.

D

Chapter Transitions

Section dividers and chapter transitions are cookie-cutter. Each chapter/lesson should establish its own mood.

E

All of the Above

Lessons should feel like distinct experiences across layout, color, components, and transitions — while staying in the SPOKES family.
