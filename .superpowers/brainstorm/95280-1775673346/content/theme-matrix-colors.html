How Color Emphasis Works

The lead color shifts: heading colors, card border accents, button backgrounds, section divider gradients, active sidebar highlights, and download buttons. The sidebar background color can also shift between --dark and --royal. All 11 brand colors remain available — the "lead" just determines which one dominates.
