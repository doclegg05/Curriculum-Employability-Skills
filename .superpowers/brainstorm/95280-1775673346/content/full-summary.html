Chapter Variation Rules

  • Each lesson assigns 2-3 card styles from the 8 options — rotated by chapter, no adjacent repeat
  • Each lesson assigns 3-4 section divider styles from the 5 options — no adjacent repeat
  • No two adjacent chapters lead with the same component type
  • Secondary accent color can shift between chapters while lead stays dominant
  • Dark Royal texture can pair with any color lead (user preference for this style)
